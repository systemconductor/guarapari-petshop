# LawStatu Trust Template

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The supplied LawStatu reference and attached page extract are the source of truth for identity, palette direction, logo, and core message. Copy is adapted for a reusable, honest legal-services template. No client-specific claims are invented.

## 1. Vibe and references

- **Vibe:** composed, credible, warm, precise.
- **Reference:** LawStatu Webflow template at https://lawstatu-wbs.webflow.io/
- **Creative decision:** preserve the LawStatu light logo, blue-gray legal palette, generous paper space, and plain-language promise while making the experience feel more trustworthy than promotional.

## 2. Scroll journey

1. **Recognition:** a calm title page says the firm protects rights with expert legal care.
2. **Orientation:** the visitor sees the firm's method, in plain steps, before being asked to contact anyone.
3. **Range:** practice areas become a readable legal index, not a sales card grid.
4. **Proof:** principles and case-style outcomes explain what careful representation looks like.
5. **Commitment:** a direct consultation request gives the visitor a clear next step and a privacy-minded expectation.

## 3. Energy curve

Quiet opening, measured middle, more focus while the visitor explores legal areas, then calm resolution. No spectacle. Trust is built by legibility and restraint.

## 4. Feeling curve

1. **Reassured:** the title page uses the reference message with ample paper space and a visible LawStatu mark.
2. **Oriented:** the method is revealed one step at a time so the visitor understands how the firm works.
3. **Curious:** the legal index shifts from a list into a selected practice area with a real explanation.
4. **Confident:** the principles and outcomes make honesty, communication, and expertise concrete.
5. **Resolved:** the contact page answers what happens next instead of pressuring the visitor.

## 5. Peak

> “I clicked one legal area, and the site explained exactly what the first conversation would cover.”

The peak lives in **chapter 3, The legal index**, where the bespoke case compass connects a selected practice area to its explanation and consultation prompt.

## 6. Signature move

**The Case Compass.** The practice-area index is a vertical legal compass. Clicking an area rotates the compass needle, updates the selected area, and carries that selection into the consultation form. Scroll also draws the compass ring and changes the active area as the visitor passes each index row. This is page-local behavior, not an engine parameter.

## 7. Aesthetic range

Editorial, restrained, and professional. The page keeps the reference's blue-gray and pale blush family, with dark ink for authority and a warm paper ground for approachability. It avoids generic black-and-gold law-firm styling.

## 8. World and assets

Distinct editorial chapters, not one unbroken world. The supplied LawStatu light logo is used locally. Practice-area imagery is downloaded from the supplied LawStatu Webflow asset URLs and retained as reference material. No generated imagery or KIE spend was used.

## Completed tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you choose the legal area that concerns you and the compass carries that choice into an honest first conversation.

## Authored silence

The opening title page has an intentional quiet zone after the promise before the first chapter begins. It is there to lower anxiety and make the method reveal feel earned, not to imply a missing asset.

## Grammar decision

**Reference-faithful legal service landing page.** The user explicitly asked for the LawStatu template's visual structure, so the second pass follows the reference instead of imposing a different scroll grammar: centered navigation, cream-and-indigo hero, coral CTA, Lady Justice hero cutout, stats strip, journey section, practice-area tabs, proof section, case highlights, team, and consultation close. ScrollCraft supplies restrained reveal/parallax behavior without changing the recognizable composition.

## Fingerprint gate

The existing root build is a dark, fixed-bar, pin/pan petshop page. This build differs on all six dimensions: chaptered editorial grammar, folio navigation, static title-page hero, short editorial flow with compass interaction, colophon close, and the Case Compass signature move.
